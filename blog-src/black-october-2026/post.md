# Black October 2026

  

In mid to late October of 2026 , Severe clusters of cyber security incidents occurred that effected almost entirety of the internet . Security researchers and threat intelligence firm ScruteX tracked over 28 distinct incidents, with a sharp concentration of critical disclosures hitting within a 48-hour window on October 20–21. AI tools were used to discover , exploit flaws and targeted payloads were used in a large scale making it a nightmare for people in charge of security.
